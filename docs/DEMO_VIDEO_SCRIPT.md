# AeroVigil narrated demo video script

The lines below are the **exact narration text** used for text-to-speech. The visuals are designed to match these eight scenes.

| Scene | Shot list | Exact narration |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Release-banner hero frame; title card; subtle zoom | **AeroVigil. A physics-guided AI that predicts wind turbine failure forty-five days before it happens.** |
| S2 | Cost-of-surprise frame with turbine art, crane/crew/lost-power callouts, big dollar number | **Inside every wind turbine, a bearing spins about a billion times over its life. When it fails by surprise, the repair means a giant crane, a specialist crew, and weeks of lost power. Up to three hundred thousand dollars. Per failure.** |
| S3 | Mock app interface: six vital-sign rows, scenario chip, big Predict button | **AeroVigil reads the vital signs every turbine already produces: vibration, temperatures, power, wind, and age. No new hardware. You just adjust the readings — or pick a scenario — and press predict.** |
| S4 | Real healthy-scenario inference: gauge + histogram + green styling | **Here's a healthy machine. The model estimates roughly two hundred and eighty days of healthy life remaining. Green: everything nominal.** |
| S5 | Real critical-scenario inference: red gauge + histogram; hot/old/shaky callouts | **Now the same turbine after months of hidden degradation. High vibration, hot bearing, seventy-eight thousand hours. The estimate collapses to four days. Critical. Act now.** |
| S6 | Tight distribution close-up with yellow 45-day line and spread callout | **And this is not one guess. It's one hundred stochastic runs of the same model. The spread is the AI admitting how sure it is. Anything left of the yellow line — forty-five days — means you can schedule a repair instead of a rescue.** |
| S7 | Safety frame: padlock icon, advisory-only banner, three short safety bullets | **And it never takes control. A fail-closed safety contract screens every output before it leaves the system. It advises humans. Humans decide.** |
| S8 | Closing scoreboard frame with metrics chips, repo URL, product name | **Ninety-four point two percent early warning accuracy. One hundred percent recall. Open source, offline-capable, and advisory-only. AeroVigil — see the failure before it happens.** |
