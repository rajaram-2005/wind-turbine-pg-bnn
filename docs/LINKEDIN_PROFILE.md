## 2. About

I grew up around wind-turbine gearboxes and learned early that renewable energy’s biggest bottleneck is not generation—it is **unplanned downtime**. A turbine can have wind, grid access, and demand; none of that matters when a bearing, gearbox, or generator failure turns a planned service visit into an urgent, expensive field operation.

My roots are in the industrial reality of wind operations. Through my family’s work in **Dynamic Wind Spares and Services**, I have been exposed to the components and maintenance decisions behind turbine availability: gearbox wear, generator health, gear oil, vibration, electrical faults, SCADA alarms, spare-part lead times, and the pressure of getting a machine safely back online. That experience shapes how I build. I am not interested in AI that looks impressive in a notebook but cannot be trusted by the engineers who must act on it.

As an **Electrical & Electronics Engineering student, deep-tech researcher, and founder**, I am building at the intersection of physical infrastructure and machine intelligence. My flagship work, **AeroVigil / `wind-turbine-pg-bnn`**, applies Physics-Guided Bayesian Neural Networks to wind-turbine health monitoring. The goal is to identify developing gearbox and generator failure patterns weeks ahead—not as a black-box score, but with a quantified uncertainty range and engineering constraints grounded in mechanical behavior.

That distinction matters. Wind-farm teams do not need another dashboard that produces a single confident number. They need decision support that communicates what the model sees, how certain it is, and whether there is enough lead time to schedule a crew, crane, parts, and a lower-impact maintenance window. By combining SCADA telemetry, industrial IoT, and physics-guided Bayesian inference, I aim to make predictive maintenance more actionable, auditable, and commercially useful.

I am working toward two connected goals: building a scalable ClimateTech company for renewable-asset reliability and contributing peer-reviewed research on trustworthy AI for clean-energy systems. My long-term horizon is international, including collaboration and market development in renewable hubs such as Australia’s growing wind-energy sector.

I welcome conversations with **wind-farm operators, O&M leaders, renewable-energy investors, AI and reliability researchers, industrial IoT builders, and mission-aligned collaborators**. If you are working to make clean-energy infrastructure more resilient, let’s connect.

**AeroVigil / `wind-turbine-pg-bnn`**
